# Match format: 5v5 on a slim 3-lane map, free 2D movement, 12–18 minutes

A Match is 5v5 on a simplified 3-lane map: towers, simple minion waves, two neutral Objectives, and no shop at first. Heroes move freely in continuous 2D as circles, with walls and brush but no height or physics. Matches target 12–18 minutes of game time. We picked this over a Teamfight Manager-style single arena because team macro (lanes, rotations, objectives) is the point of the league. We kept the map slim and the matches short so 10 Heroes stay readable on a phone and a whole VOD is watchable.

## Considered Options

- 3v3 on 2 lanes: easier to read, but too few Roles for interesting drafts and macro.
- A grid or hex board: simpler sim, but no skillshots or dodging, and it looks like a board game.
