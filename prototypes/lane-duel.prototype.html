Lane Duel Lab

The question: can a sim that knows nothing about player skill, driven by bots that only see what their team sees and only send orders back, produce a 1v1 lane duel that looks like two humans of different skill, without either bot getting information it shouldn't have?

What's real here and what's simplified
  • Real (from the decisions so far): 20 ticks per second. Each bot gets only its team's Vision and returns Intents. Player Ratings live only inside the bot. The sim refuses illegal orders. Abilities are an Ability Type plus Effects. Bots decide 10 times a second.
  • Simplified: one lane, no minions, towers can't be destroyed, both players use the same hero (a mirror match, so ratings are the only difference), and the Team Brain is skipped because the Assignment is fixed: "hold mid lane".
  • The hero, Lumen: auto attack (range 430, 34 damage), Spark Bolt (Projectile: 95 damage + 35% slow for 1.5 s, 6 s cooldown, a 0.25 s wind-up you can see), Skip (Dash, 300 units, 10 s cooldown).

    What to judge

    1. Do the duels look like two people playing? Does turning each rating up or down make a visible difference?

    3. When a bot orders an attack on someone who just walked out of sight, the sim refuses. Is that right, or should the order become "walk to where I last saw them"?
