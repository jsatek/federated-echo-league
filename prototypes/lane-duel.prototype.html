Lane Duel Lab

The question: if each bot holds a Stance for a few seconds (farm and punish, shove and reset, all-in…) instead of re-picking its single best move 10 times a second, does the lane look like two people with intentions: farming first, trading on purpose, dropping aggro in the brush?

What's new in v3 and v2, and what's still simplified
  • v3, Stances: each bot commits to a Stance for at least 3 s, and only urgent events end it early: about to die, their escape just went down, the tower or several minions are hitting me. Decision-making noise is rolled once per Stance call. Actions inside a Stance carry no noise, so they don't twitch.
  • v3, matchups: each Hero's range, escape, engage reach and poke are read from its abilities. Ranged against melee leans towards farming and punishing, spaced around the enemy's engage timer. Melee against ranged respects them until their escape is down.
  • v3, brush and aggro: minions and towers lose track of a Hero they can't see, so stepping into the brush drops aggro. Bots can see what's hitting them and use that.
  • v3, recall: a bot recalls when nobody can see and reach it during the 4 s channel. Otherwise it walks back first, and never stands frozen.
  • Stakes: a Minion Wave every 15 s. Gold comes only from Last Hits and kills, and is spent automatically on upgrades when you reach base. XP takes you to level 6. Towers have health and can fall. Minions and towers turn on any Hero who hits an enemy Hero near them.
  • Two Heroes: Warden (melee: Charge = Dash + 1 s stun, Cleave = cone, Brace = shield) and Lumen (ranged: Spark Bolt = projectile + slow, which minions block; Flash Freeze = delayed circle + 1 s stun; Blink). Every ability is an Ability Type plus Effects, run by the same generic code.
  • Brush on both sides of the lane: bots can lurk for an ambush, slip in to break a chase, avoid walking past brush where an enemy probably is, or face-check it if their Positioning is poor.
  • Still simplified: one lane, no jungle, no Team Brain (the Assignment is fixed), and waves come faster than in a real match so it's watchable. Lane Priority is shown, but leaving to help another lane needs other lanes, so that comes later.
